SALES ORDER POLICY

1. Order Processing
Sales orders are normally processed within 1 business day after confirmation.
Orders in "Processing" status are being prepared for fulfillment.

2. Shipping
Once a sales order is shipped, the customer can track the shipment using the tracking information provided by the company.

3. Returns
Customers can request a return within 30 days of delivery.
The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

4. Refunds
Approved returns are eligible for a refund according to the company's return policy.
Refund processing normally takes 5 to 7 business days after the returned product is approved.

5. Order Cancellation
A sales order can be cancelled before it is shipped.
Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is generally not available.
